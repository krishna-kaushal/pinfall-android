# Privacy Policy — Pinfall Rescue

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Pinfall Rescue is a fully offline puzzle game. This policy describes what the
app does with your information — in short: nothing leaves your device.

## Data we collect

None. The app:

- does not ask for, access, or transmit any personal information;
- does not use the internet (it requests no network permission);
- does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs;
- does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or files.

## Data stored on your device

Your level progress (which levels you have completed) is stored locally on your
device using the app's own storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it is
deleted if you uninstall the app.

## Children

The game contains no chat, no purchases, and no links to external websites,
and is suitable for general audiences.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL
with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Play Store
listing.
