# Privacy Policy — Pinfall Rescue

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Pinfall Rescue is a pull-the-pin puzzle game. The game itself works offline:
your level progress never leaves your device. The only network use in the
app is the banner ad at the bottom of the screen (see Advertising below).

## Data the game itself collects

None. The game:

- does not ask for, access, or transmit any personal information;
- does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or files;
- has no accounts, no chat, and no in-app purchases.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and
(where allowed) personalise ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your
device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about ad views and
interactions. Google handles that data under its own policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings or at
https://adssettings.google.com. The developer receives no data that
identifies you.

## Permissions

The app requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, used only so AdMob can
load ads. The game itself needs no connection.

## Data stored on your device

Your level progress (which levels you have completed) is stored locally on
your device using the app's own storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and
it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

## Children

The game contains no chat, no purchases, and no links to external websites,
and is suitable for general audiences.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL
with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Play Store
listing.
